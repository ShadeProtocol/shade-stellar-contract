# Get a refund

For an invoice payment, contact the merchant that issued the invoice. The merchant reviews the request and, when approved, sends the refund to the wallet used for the payment.

<a id="refunds"></a>

## What to do

1. Send the merchant your invoice or order details and the wallet address you paid from.
2. Ask whether the refund is approved and when it will be submitted.
3. Once the merchant confirms it was sent, check that wallet for the refund.

Refunds are sent on Stellar and appear after the refund transaction is confirmed. Network confirmation and merchant review take different amounts of time, so Shade does not set a fixed refund arrival time.

The merchant's normal invoice refund window is seven days from payment, so contact them promptly. If an invoice expired after you paid all or part of it, ask the merchant about the available refund path. Refunds for subscriptions and tickets follow their own plan or event terms, so check with the merchant or organizer.
