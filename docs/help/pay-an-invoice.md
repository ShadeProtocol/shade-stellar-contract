# Pay an invoice with a Stellar wallet

Use the wallet you want to pay from, and check the payment details before approving.

<a id="pay-an-invoice"></a>

## Steps

1. Open the invoice link from the merchant.
2. Check the merchant name, amount, token, and any expiry shown.
3. Connect your Stellar wallet. If asked, choose the account and network shown by the merchant's checkout.
4. Review the wallet request. Approve it only if the amount and destination match the invoice.
5. Keep the checkout open until it confirms payment. If it does not update, refresh the invoice status before trying again.

If you do not have enough of the requested token to cover the invoice and network fee, add funds before retrying. Some assets also need to be enabled in your wallet before you can receive or send them.

If your wallet says the payment succeeded but the invoice still looks unpaid, do not pay again immediately. Send the merchant your wallet address and transaction link or ID so they can check it.
