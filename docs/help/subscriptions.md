# Manage or cancel a subscription

Your merchant's checkout or account page is the place to review your subscription and stop future charges.

<a id="subscriptions"></a>

## Steps

1. Open the merchant's subscription or billing page and connect the wallet you used to subscribe.
2. Review the plan, charge amount, and billing interval.
3. Choose **Cancel subscription** and approve the wallet request if one appears.
4. Check for a cancellation confirmation. Contact the merchant if you cannot find the subscription or cancellation option.

When you first subscribe, review any wallet request for repeat charges; check the asset and amount before approving. Keep enough of the selected asset for scheduled charges.

Cancellation stops future subscription charges once it is confirmed. It does not always return money for time already paid; any unused-time refund depends on the subscription service and the merchant's terms. If a charge fails, contact the merchant to check your balance and payment authorization before trying again.
