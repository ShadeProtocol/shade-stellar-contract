# Transfer, resell, or get a ticket refund

Ticket options depend on the organizer's ticketing service. Use the ticket link or account page provided by the organizer.

<a id="tickets"></a>

## Steps

1. Open your ticket and check for **Transfer**, **Resell**, or **Refund**.
2. To transfer, enter the recipient's wallet address and confirm the ticket details.
3. To resell, use the official resale option, review the price, and approve the wallet request. The organizer may receive a portion of the resale price.
4. To request a refund, follow the event's refund terms or contact the organizer.

Some tickets cannot be transferred after check-in. A ticket refund may only be available if the organizer enables it or cancels the event; resale is not the same as a refund. If an event is cancelled and your ticket has not been refunded, contact the organizer with your ticket details.
