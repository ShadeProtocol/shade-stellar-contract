# Why a payment failed

Check the invoice and your wallet before trying again. A failed payment attempt normally does not complete the transfer.

<a id="payment-failed"></a>

## Common reasons

- **Not enough funds:** your wallet needs enough of the invoice token for the remaining amount, plus any network fee.
- **Asset not enabled:** some Stellar assets need a trustline, which lets your wallet hold that asset. Use your wallet's asset controls or ask the merchant which asset to add.
- **Invoice expired:** ask the merchant for a new invoice. Do not keep retrying an expired link.
- **Payments temporarily paused:** the service may be paused for maintenance or safety. Wait and try again later, or contact the merchant.
- **Wrong account or network:** return to checkout and select the Stellar account and network it requests.

After correcting the issue, reload checkout and confirm the invoice is still payable before approving another request. If your wallet shows a confirmed transaction but the invoice did not update, share the transaction ID with the merchant instead of paying again.
