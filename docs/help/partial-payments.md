# Make or finish a partial payment

Some invoices let you pay less than the full amount first and pay the rest later.

<a id="partial-payments"></a>

## Steps

1. Check the invoice's remaining balance in checkout.
2. Choose an amount no greater than that balance and approve it in your wallet.
3. Wait for confirmation, then reload the invoice to see the updated balance.
4. Pay the remaining balance using the same wallet that made the first payment.

Shade does not accept more than the unpaid balance on an invoice. If the invoice has expired or checkout does not offer another payment, contact the merchant. If another wallet made the first payment, ask the merchant how to proceed; the invoice may be linked to that first payer.
